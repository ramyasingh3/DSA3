# Combination Sum II

## Problem
Unique combinations summing to target (candidates once).

## Example
```
Input: [-4, 18, 10, 29, 37, 26, -8, 30, 42, 40, 1]
Output: (compute according to problem statement)
```

## Constraints
- Reasonable input sizes for interview settings (`n` up to ~10^5 unless noted)
